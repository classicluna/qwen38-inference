Verdict

Keep UD-Q3_K_XL as the default. It leaves 1.50 GiB of free VRAM at 32k context versus 0.53 GiB for UD-IQ4_XS, and decodes 4.4% faster — with prefill unchanged.

Switch to UD-IQ4_XS when quality matters more than that margin — it is 6.8% better on perplexity — and either accept 0.53 GiB of headroom at 32k, or run it at 16k context (14.86 GiB peak, 1.12 GiB free) if the desktop is busy. Below 0.7 GiB free, an amdgpu VRAM overflow would spill to system RAM, and this box only has 15 GiB of RAM to absorb that.
